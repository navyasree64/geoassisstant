# Data pack: what is real and what is synthetic (disclosure)

REAL: school building polygons, osm_id and names in `data/east_india.gpkg` layer `schools` (OpenStreetMap, from the supplied schools.gpkg, cut to the Kolkata-Howrah box lon 88.2-88.5, lat 22.45-22.70, 81 features).

SYNTHETIC (generated by `build_dataset.py`, seed 42, written for this assessment):
- board, seats, cut-off marks and locality columns of the schools layer (30 "curated" schools; locality = nearest of 15 hand-placed locality centres)
- layers `roads` (5 polylines through real school locations, so `near` has matches) and `landuse` (15 rectangles)
- the 14 documents in `data/docs/` (12 circulars, 1 guideline, 1 geography note); 2 schools in the documents (Sunrise International School, Lakeview Public School) deliberately do not exist in the layer
- all evaluation files in `eval/` (gold entities with exact character spans, 30 retrieval queries with graded judgments, 40 calibration questions, 10 dev questions)

Chunk IDs in the judgments follow paragraph chunking, e.g. `CIR-006-table` (see `eval/chunks_reference.jsonl`, with character spans per chunk).
No assessor-supplied documents or evaluation files exist (confirmed by phone on 13 Oct 2026).
