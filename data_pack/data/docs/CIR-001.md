# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/001
Date: 10 January 2026
Subject: Admission schedule for WBBSE schools in the district

This circular applies to all schools affiliated with the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education (WBBSE). Application forms for Class V admission will be available from 1 February 2026 and the last date for submission is 28 February 2026. The admission lottery will be held on 15 March 2026.

A child must be at least 9 years old on 1 January 2026 to apply for Class V. The age limit for Class I admission is 5 years. Schools such as Hare School, Bethune Collegiate School and Tantia High School must display the schedule on their notice boards.

Parents may submit queries to the District Education Office in Howrah or Salkia. Under Rule 3 of the district admission guidelines, no school may charge a fee for the application form.
