# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/002
Date: 15 January 2026
Subject: Seat matrix and age limit for CBSE schools

This circular applies to all schools affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE). The minimum age for Class I admission is 6 years as on 31 March 2026. Admissions follow the lottery described in the district lottery guidelines.

Approved seats for Class I:
| School | Seats |
|---|---|
| Delhi Public School | 150 |
| Don Bosco School | 120 |
| Heritage Academy | 90 |
| Narain Dass Bangur Memorial Multipurpose School | 100 |
| Nava Nalanda Group of Schools | 80 |

Heritage Academy and Nava Nalanda Group of Schools must complete registration by 31 January 2026.
