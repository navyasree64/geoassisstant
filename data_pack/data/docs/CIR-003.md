# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/003
Date: 20 January 2026
Subject: Seat matrix and age limit for ICSE schools

This circular applies to all schools affiliated with the Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (ICSE). The minimum age for Class I admission is 5 years. Admissions follow the lottery described in the district lottery guidelines.

Approved seats for Class I:
| School | Seats |
|---|---|
| Saint Xavier's Collegiate School | 90 |
| St. Jude's High School | 70 |
| Holy Cross School | 80 |
| The Calcutta Emmanuel School | 60 |
| Auxilium Convent School | 50 |
| Harrow Hall | 40 |

The new ICSE school Sunrise International School in Salt Lake has been allotted 55 seats. St Xavier's Collegiate School will publish its registration list by 10 February 2026.
