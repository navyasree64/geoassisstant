# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/004
Date: 5 February 2026
Subject: Revised admission schedule for WBBSE schools

This circular supersedes Circular DEO/ADM/2026/001 dated 10 January 2026.

For WBBSE schools the last date for Class V application is extended to 10 March 2026 and the admission lottery will be held on 25 March 2026. All other conditions, including the age limit of 9 years, remain unchanged.
