# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/005
Date: 12 February 2026
Subject: Reservation for economically weaker sections (EWS)

This circular applies to all schools affiliated with CBSE, ICSE and WBBSE. Under Rule 4(1), 25 percent of seats in Class I must be reserved for children from economically weaker sections (EWS).

Under Rule 5, an applicant must submit an income certificate showing annual family income below Rs. 120000. Schools such as Delhi Public School and Don Bosco School must publish the number of EWS seats on their websites.
