# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/006
Date: 20 February 2026
Subject: Class V seats and cut-off marks for WBBSE boys' schools

This circular applies to WBBSE boys' schools. It lists the seats for Class V and the cut-off marks in the admission test (out of 100).

Seats and cut-off marks:
| School | Seats | Cut-off marks |
|---|---|---|
| Hare School | 120 | 78 |
| Tantia High School | 90 | 72 |
| B.K.Paul's Institution | 100 | 70 |
| Baranagar Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama High School | 80 | 80 |
| Chittaranjan Boys High School | 60 | 65 |
| Dinabandhu Institution Main | 70 | 62 |
| Dum Dum Baidyanath Institution | 110 | 68 |
| Vivekananda School | 60 | 60 |

Baranagar Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama High School has the highest cut-off (80 marks) and Hare School has the most seats (120 seats).
