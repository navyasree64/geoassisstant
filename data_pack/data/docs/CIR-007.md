# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/007
Date: 22 February 2026
Subject: Class V seats and cut-off marks for WBBSE girls' schools

This circular applies to WBBSE girls' schools. It lists the seats for Class V and the cut-off marks in the admission test (out of 100).

Seats and cut-off marks:
| School | Seats | Cut-off marks |
|---|---|---|
| Bethune Collegiate School | 100 | 82 |
| Chittaranjan Girls High School | 80 | 70 |
| Hindu Girl's School | 90 | 75 |
| Kamala Chatterjee Girls School | 70 | 68 |
| Debiswari Girl's High School | 60 | 60 |
| Rajarhat Shiksha Niketan High School for Girls | 70 | 66 |

Bethune Collegiate School has the highest cut-off (82 marks) and the most seats (100 seats).
