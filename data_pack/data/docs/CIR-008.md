# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/008
Date: 1 March 2026
Subject: Mid-session transfer admissions

Under Rule 7, a student seeking transfer must produce a transfer certificate issued by the previous school. Vacancies must be filled within 30 days of the notice.

This circular applies to schools affiliated with CBSE, ICSE and WBBSE. Dum Dum Krishna Kumar Hindu Academy and Pandit Motilal Institute have reported vacancies.
