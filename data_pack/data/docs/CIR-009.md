# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/009
Date: 5 March 2026
Subject: Road safety near schools

This circular applies to schools affiliated with CBSE, ICSE and WBBSE. Schools located on the following roads must install speed breakers and zebra crossings within 45 days.

Schools on Barrackpore Trunk Road: Baranagar Ramakrishna Mission Ashrama High School, Sri Ramkrishna Vidyasram, Dum Dum Krishna Kumar Hindu Academy, Dum Dum Baidyanath Institution, Auxilium Convent School.

Schools on AJC Bose Road: The Calcutta Emmanuel School, Chittaranjan Girls High School, Kamala Chatterjee Girls School, Chittaranjan Boys High School, Nava Nalanda Group of Schools.

Schools on Grand Trunk Road: Delhi Public School, Rajarhat Shiksha Niketan High School for Boys, Rajarhat Shiksha Niketan High School for Girls, Narain Dass Bangur Memorial Multipurpose School, Pandit Motilal Institute.

Schools on Diamond Harbour Road: St. Jude's High School, Debiswari Girl's High School, Bethune Collegiate School, Hare School, Tantia High School.

Schools on Belgachia Road: Hindu Girl's School, B.K.Paul's Institution, Dinabandhu Institution Main, Vivekananda School, Heritage Academy.
