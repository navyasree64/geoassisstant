# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/011
Date: 18 March 2026
Subject: Revised income ceiling for EWS applicants

This circular supersedes Circular DEO/ADM/2026/005 and applies to schools affiliated with CBSE, ICSE and WBBSE.

Under Rule 5 (as amended), the annual family income ceiling is revised to Rs. 150000.

Rule 4(1) is unchanged: 25 percent of Class I seats remain reserved for EWS children.
