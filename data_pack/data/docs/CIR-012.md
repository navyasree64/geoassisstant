# Circular No. DEO/ADM/2026/012
Date: 2 April 2026
Subject: Revised seat matrix for CBSE schools

This circular supersedes Circular DEO/ADM/2026/002 dated 15 January 2026 and applies to CBSE schools.

Revised seats for Class I:
| School | Seats |
|---|---|
| Delhi Public School | 160 |
| Don Bosco School | 120 |
| Heritage Academy | 100 |
| Narain Dass Bangur Memorial Multipurpose School | 100 |
| Nava Nalanda Group of Schools | 90 |
| Lakeview Public School | 70 |

The new CBSE school Lakeview Public School has been allotted 70 seats. Delhi Public School has the most seats (160 seats).
